01

Project overview

Company background

An MBA business consulting project at the University of New Brunswick examined CAA Atlantic’s value proposition for New Canadians in Atlantic Canada.

Industry context

Membership & mobility services

Project background

How can CAA Atlantic make its membership more relevant and accessible to New Canadians?

Scope of the analysis

Current offerings, newcomer needs, demographic and competitive analysis, value gaps, target audiences, messages, channels and implementation planning.

Tools & technologies

PowerPoint

Business value

Evidence-based recommendations to improve awareness, relevance and engagement within newcomer communities.

Portfolio project summary

Translating newcomer research into relevant member value, community partnerships and a phased market-development strategy.

02

Business challenges

  • Awareness and understanding of CAA membership vary among newcomers.
  • Price, unclear value, language barriers and car ownership affect perceived relevance.
  • Different newcomer segments need different combinations of products, communication and support.
03

Business analysis

How can CAA Atlantic make its membership more relevant and accessible to New Canadians?

Current offerings, newcomer needs, demographic and competitive analysis, value gaps, target audiences, messages, channels and implementation planning.

04

Project objectives

Identify which CAA products and benefits resonate with New Canadians, where gaps remain, and how the organization can reach these audiences.

05

Evidence & preparation

Primary and secondary research informed needs assessment, segmentation, personas, customer journeys and competitive benchmarking. Survey findings are presented separately in the analytics case study.

06

Analysis & solution design

01

Listen to newcomer needs

Combined target-audience research with a review of CAA products, services and benefits.

02

Make audience differences tangible

Developed five personas spanning families, international students, refugees, senior parents and temporary workers.

03

Find value and access gaps

Compared customer needs, membership value and competing options across the customer journey.

04

Turn insight into a phased plan

Developed newcomer offers, partnership channels and short-, mid- and long-term recommendations.

07

Insight generation

  • A single membership message does not reflect the needs of all newcomer groups.
  • Clear value communication and trusted community channels can address awareness and access gaps.
  • Relevant bundles and practical support can connect roadside, travel, insurance and rewards benefits to life-stage needs.

09

Recommendations

  • Pilot a newcomer trial and segment-specific membership offers.
  • Build a clearer newcomer landing page with accessible, relevant value explanations.
  • Develop partnerships with newcomer organizations and community channels.
  • Expand successful initiatives using awareness, engagement and conversion evidence.
10

Strategic recommendation

Lead with relevant newcomer value and trusted partnerships, then scale through a measured two-year implementation plan.

How success would be measured

  • Newcomer awareness and qualified engagement
  • Membership acquisition and conversion
  • Use of services and member satisfaction
  • Community partnerships and referral activity
Evidence & outcome status

Research-led recommendations and a proposed two-year plan. Growth targets and expected impacts are planning measures, not reported results.
