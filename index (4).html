Project overview

Company background

Peakline Logistics is a freight and supply-chain case study spanning transportation, warehousing, distribution, last-mile delivery and customs brokerage across Nigerian regions.

Industry context

Logistics & supply chain

Project background

Where is operational friction preventing shipment demand from translating into efficient delivery?

Scope of the analysis

Revenue, freight types, customer segments, delivery performance, route bottlenecks, warehouses and workforce allocation.

Tools & technologies

Power BI, SQL

Business value

A clearer view of where route, hub and resource decisions can improve operational execution.

Portfolio project summary

Exploring delivery delays, regional operations, warehouse demand and workforce utilization to focus improvement efforts.

02

Business challenges

  • Recurring route delays put service quality and customer trust under pressure.
  • High-volume regions and major warehouses face capacity and execution strain.
  • Workforce allocation varies across regions, requiring closer productivity review.
03

Business analysis

Where is operational friction preventing shipment demand from translating into efficient delivery?

Revenue, freight types, customer segments, delivery performance, route bottlenecks, warehouses and workforce allocation.

04

Project objectives

Analyze delivery and operational performance to prioritize route, warehouse and workforce improvements.

05

Evidence & preparation

The performance presentation brings together executive, operations-and-delivery, and customer-and-staff reporting. Delivery KPI definitions need reconciliation before setting numerical delay-reduction targets.

06

Exploratory analysis

01

Frame the business problem

Linked commercial performance to execution, fulfillment cost and delivery reliability.

02

Inspect delivery patterns

Compared on-time and delayed shipments by region and looked for recurring route bottlenecks.

03

Connect demand and capacity

Reviewed warehouse volume, customer contribution and regional workload.

04

Prioritize operational decisions

Translated recurring patterns into route, hub and predictive-analytics recommendations.

07

Insight generation

  • Lagos and Oyo combine high shipment activity with delivery pressure.
  • Anambra-linked routes recur in the bottleneck analysis, making them useful candidates for route review.
  • Regional staff-to-shipment ratios indicate where utilization and productivity need further investigation.

Improvement recommendations

Route optimization, regional micro-hubs and predictive delay analysis.

09

Recommendations

  • Prioritize a route-optimization pilot on recurring bottleneck routes.
  • Assess regional micro-hubs against demand, warehouse capacity and service needs.
  • Explore predictive delay analytics after agreeing delivery definitions and validating input data.
10

Strategic recommendation

Concentrate improvement on execution efficiency, using connected route, warehouse and workforce evidence.

How success would be measured

  • Agreed on-time-delivery and delay definitions
  • Delay patterns by route and region
  • Warehouse workload and capacity
  • Regional workforce utilization
Evidence & outcome status

Completed performance analysis with proposed operational actions. The presentation contains inconsistent delivery percentages, so this hub uses qualitative findings and does not reproduce those figures.
