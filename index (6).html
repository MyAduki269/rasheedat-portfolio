01

Project overview

Company background

Zerling Trust Bank is a banking case study covering retail, commercial and digital services. Management needs better visibility into inconsistent branch performance, growing transaction volumes and dormant accounts.

Industry context

Banking & financial services

Project background

What can operational banking data reveal about branch performance, customer engagement and account health?

Scope of the analysis

Six connected data entities and seven reporting perspectives: executive, branch, customer, accounts, transactions, loans and employees.

Tools & technologies

PostgreSQL, SQL, Power BI, DAX

Business value

A repeatable analytical foundation for monitoring performance and identifying where management should investigate or act.

Portfolio project summary

An end-to-end BI case study connecting customer, account, transaction, loan, branch and workforce performance.

02

Business challenges

  • Branch performance varies without a consistent view of operational KPIs.
  • Dormant accounts and customer-service pressure require closer investigation.
  • Transaction and loan activity need clearer, connected reporting.
03

Business analysis

What can operational banking data reveal about branch performance, customer engagement and account health?

Six connected data entities and seven reporting perspectives: executive, branch, customer, accounts, transactions, loans and employees.

04

Project objectives

Transform operational data into SQL analytical views and Power BI dashboards that help management monitor performance and prioritize improvements.

05

Evidence & preparation

Customer, account, transaction, loan, branch and employee data were organized in PostgreSQL. SQL views provide analytical outputs, with relationships modeled for Power BI reporting.

06

Exploratory analysis

01

Prepare the data foundation

Organized banking entities and relationships in a PostgreSQL relational model.

02

Answer business questions in SQL

Used analytical views, CTEs, window functions and running totals to investigate operational performance.

03

Build the reporting layer

Connected PostgreSQL to Power BI and developed KPI cards, trends, rankings and portfolio views.

04

Turn reporting into decisions

Connected each dashboard perspective to management questions and recommended follow-up actions.

07

Insight generation

  • Branch comparisons help identify differences in customer, account, transaction and loan activity.
  • Active and dormant account views make engagement opportunities easier to identify.
  • Channel, transaction-trend and loan-distribution views support more focused operational oversight.

09

Recommendations

  • Use targeted engagement to investigate and reactivate dormant accounts.
  • Compare branches consistently and assess practices at stronger-performing locations.
  • Make executive KPI, transaction and loan monitoring part of a regular management review.
10

Strategic recommendation

Use an integrated SQL-to-Power BI reporting layer to move from isolated operational records to consistent management decisions.

How success would be measured

  • Active versus dormant accounts
  • Branch performance and transaction trends
  • Customer engagement and deposit concentration
  • Loan portfolio mix and reporting consistency
Evidence & outcome status

Completed analytics case study. Business benefits describe intended decision value; no post-implementation improvement is claimed.
