01

Project overview

Company background

Robphil Energy manages employee journeys through Fleet & Logistics. Requests begin in an existing Travel Management Portal, but approvals, fleet coordination and journey records rely on fragmented manual handoffs.

Industry context

Energy & fleet operations

Project background

How can an existing travel portal connect to a safer, more visible downstream fleet process?

Scope of the analysis

The downstream journey lifecycle: approvals, Security clearance, sign-offs, driver and vehicle assignment, inspection, manifests, journey status and records.

Tools & technologies

PowerPoint

Business value

A traceable requirements and process foundation for a centralized Fleet Management Platform integrated with the existing travel application.

Portfolio project summary

Redesigning manual fleet handoffs into an integrated journey workflow with traceable approvals and centralized records.

02

Business challenges

  • Digital travel requests become manual schedules, disconnected approvals and paper records.
  • Fleet planning, inspections and manifests lack a connected end-to-end view.
  • Separate records make journey status and management oversight difficult.
03

Business analysis

How can an existing travel portal connect to a safer, more visible downstream fleet process?

The downstream journey lifecycle: approvals, Security clearance, sign-offs, driver and vehicle assignment, inspection, manifests, journey status and records.

04

Project objectives

Analyze current-state gaps and define a connected future-state fleet workflow while retaining the required approval and safety controls.

05

Evidence & preparation

The capstone documents 11 current-state stages, three control decisions and 11 gaps. Requirements are traced from business problems through root causes, future-state changes and expected value.

06

Analysis & solution design

01

Map the current journey

Captured actors, handoffs and approval decisions across the 11-stage AS-IS workflow.

02

Explain the gaps

Applied gap analysis and fishbone/root-cause thinking to process, technology, people and visibility issues.

03

Design the connected workflow

Defined an integrated Fleet platform with digital approvals, assignment, inspection, manifests and status records.

04

Trace and prioritize requirements

Developed the BRD, functional and non-functional requirements, MoSCoW priorities and an implementation roadmap.

07

Insight generation

  • The main gap is a disconnected downstream fleet process; a travel-request portal already exists.
  • Digitization should preserve manager approval, Security clearance, required sign-offs and vehicle-fitness checks.
  • Journey-monitoring integration and detailed service targets require stakeholder and technical validation.

09

Recommendations

  • Integrate journey information from the Travel Management application into the Fleet platform.
  • Digitize approval, inspection and passenger records while retaining all control decisions.
  • Validate tracking integration, availability, performance and recovery needs before solution sizing.
10

Strategic recommendation

Implement a centralized Fleet Management Platform around a connected, controlled journey lifecycle.

How success would be measured

  • Journey processing time and approval visibility
  • Digital-record completeness and paper usage
  • User adoption and workflow adherence
  • Integration reliability and journey traceability
Evidence & outcome status

Proposed solution and implementation roadmap. Baselines, numerical targets and realized benefits require validation; no deployment outcome is claimed.
